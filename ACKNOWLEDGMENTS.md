# Acknowledgments

Open Masorah v0.1 builds on the work of others, gratefully acknowledged. These
are courtesy citations and provenance notes; for the **rights status** of each
component, see [`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

## UXLC / Tanach.us

The consonantal text for folios F119A, F119B, and F120A was sourced from the
Unicode/XML Leningrad Codex (UXLC) 2.5, transcribed and maintained by Tanach.us
Inc. ([tanach.us](https://tanach.us/)). We gratefully acknowledge their
digitization work; Tanach.us asks that citation to the site as the source of the
text be made, and we gladly do so.

## West Semitic Research Project (WSRP)

The consonantal text of folio F118B was hand-transcribed by Open Masorah from
color photographs of the Leningrad Codex produced by the West Semitic Research
Project (B. Zuckerman et al.). No photographs are redistributed; the dataset
provides IIIF links for viewing only. Rights in the photographs are asserted by
WSRP; consult the rights holder before reusing the images. (An Internet Archive
copy carries a Public Domain Mark applied by an uploader, not by WSRP.)

## Base manuscript

The Leningrad Codex (Firkovich MS Evr. I B19a, National Library of Russia;
Samuel ben Jacob, ca. 1008 CE) is the base manuscript. Its text is in the public
domain.
